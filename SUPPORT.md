# Support

## Questions and discussion

Open a [discussion](../../discussions) for anything that is not a defect —
how to deploy it, whether it fits your practice, how to adapt the clinical
reference for your region.

## Bugs

Open an [issue](../../issues/new/choose). The most valuable report this project
can receive is a **reproducible example of clinically wrong output**: the input
you gave, and what came back. You do not need to know why it happened.

Invent the patient. Never paste real patient data into an issue, even
de-identified.

## Security

See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Report privately, not in an issue.

## What this project cannot help with

- **Whether you may use this on real patients.** That is a question for your
  regulator and your indemnity insurer, not a maintainer.
- **HIPAA compliance.** The software implements technical safeguards; compliance
  is organisational and belongs to your practice. See the README.
- **Clinical decisions.** This system supports them and does not make them.
